# Archived submission notes

These notes belong to an earlier exploratory manuscript on geospatial site selection for underground hydrogen storage.

The manuscript is not the current research direction of this repository and these notes should not be used to submit the paper in its present form. In particular, the old abstract, claims about site suitability, PINN implementation, numerical results, author metadata and journal recommendations have not been revalidated as part of the current research programme.

The current project focuses on hydrogen dissolution, retention and loss in water-bearing geological formations. Its central factors are brine chemistry, pore confinement and mineral-fluid interactions.

For the current research direction, see:

- [Main project README](../README.md)
- [Research vision](../research/research_vision.md)
- [Project charter and decision log](../research/project_charter_and_decision_log.md)

The original manuscript files and figures are retained in this folder for historical reference only.
