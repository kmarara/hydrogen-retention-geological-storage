# H₂ solubility observation schema

**Status:** schema specification; no canonical observations released.  
**Applies to:** experimental H₂ dissolution / aqueous-phase solubility records only.

## Unit of observation

One row represents one reported equilibrium measurement of hydrogen dissolved in a specified aqueous liquid phase at a defined temperature, pressure and brine composition. A row must not represent an IFT value, adsorption amount, diffusion coefficient, model prediction or molecular-simulation output.

## Controlled evidence labels

- `experimental_table`: value transcribed from an original experimental table.
- `experimental_supplementary`: value transcribed from primary supplementary information.
- `experimental_digitised`: value digitised from a plot; preserve calibration and digitisation uncertainty.
- `derived_from_experiment`: computed from reported experimental values; formula and inputs required.
- `correlation_generated`: calculated from a fitted or published correlation.
- `thermodynamic_model`: generated by an equation of state or thermodynamic model.
- `molecular_simulation`: generated by molecular simulation.
- `secondary_compilation`: copied from a review or secondary database without primary row-level verification.
- `unknown`: provenance not established.

Only eligible primary experimental observations may enter the primary experimental dataset. Digitised values, derived values and model outputs must remain explicitly labelled and separately analysed.

## Non-negotiable comparison rules

1. Do not mix mol H₂/kg water with liquid mole fraction or salt-free mole fraction in one unlabelled target column.
2. Do not assume total pressure is interchangeable with H₂ partial pressure or fugacity.
3. Do not assume a reported molality is mol/kg solution; molality is conventionally expressed per kg solvent, but retain the source's exact definition.
4. Preserve salt identity and the full brine recipe. NaCl-only brine is not interchangeable with mixed or natural formation brine.
5. Do not infer missing brine chemistry from a study's title, another paper or a model.
6. Do not treat a fitted-model average absolute deviation as experimental measurement uncertainty.
7. Do not interpret an expanded uncertainty with coverage factor k=2 as a one-standard-deviation uncertainty.
8. Do not count repeated observations reproduced in later papers as independent experiments without lineage review.
9. Keep H₂ solubility separate from IFT, adsorption and diffusion.
10. A bulk aqueous solubility measurement does not directly validate a confined nanopore simulation unless the physical system and measured property are comparable.

## Release gates

Before a canonical release:
- original paper and SI available;
- exact source coordinates captured for every row;
- original value, unit, basis and conditions preserved;
- chemistry and pressure definitions reviewed;
- uncertainty information recorded without reinterpretation;
- conversions unit-tested;
- duplicates and reused experiments reviewed;
- experimental records separated from correlations, simulations and secondary compilations;
- CSV snapshot checksummed and documented.
